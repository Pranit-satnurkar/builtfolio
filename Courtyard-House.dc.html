Design Narrative

Passive Climate Design on a Bavdhan Hillside

Oriented east-west to capture prevailing monsoon breezes, the Courtyard House reinterprets the traditional Maharashtra Wada for a contemporary urban family. A sunken open-to-sky central courtyard serves as both the social heart and a natural thermal chimney, venting hot air upward while drawing cooler air through shaded basalt stone verandas.

Local Deccan basalt was quarried within 20 km of the site, paired with exposed board-marked concrete slabs and locally sourced teak joinery. Rainwater from the overhanging roof is directed to a 35,000-liter subterranean cistern.
